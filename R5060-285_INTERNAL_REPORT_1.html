The overstatement is worth money. At $2.9M, a buyer paying for 17.8 acres is really paying $204,369/acre for 14.19 acres — not the $162,921/acre the stated figure implies. The true per-acre price is 25% higher than the stated acreage suggests (equivalently, the stated figure understates it by 20%). How the carve-out is modelled, and why it flatters the site. The retained homesite is modelled as a 1.5-acre area centred on R5060 040's parcel centroid (not on the surveyed dwelling location, which was not obtained). Sellable acreage is placement-independent — any 1.5 acres removed from 15.69 leaves 14.19. What placement does change is how encumbered the remainder is: as modelled, 0.75 of the 1.5 acres falls inside the stream buffer, so the carve-out absorbs part of the encumbrance and the balance reads cleaner than it should. Across realistic placements the practical developable base runs 9.05–10.37 acres, against the 9.67 acres carried in this report. The error direction is pro-seller — correcting it makes the site worse, so no conclusion here depends on it. Whether Snellville will even permit the subdivision that creates the carve-out must be confirmed with the City, and a retained occupied parcel inside a development tract is a permanent interior out-parcel a production builder will discount for.

The honest reading, and it cuts both ways. $2.9M does clear if all three modelled inputs land at the top of their bands at once — and it clears at 4.31 u/ac, below the 4.62 u/ac Snellville actually approved at Lenora Church in 2025. It also clears at market pricing if the lot ratio is 24% or horizontal cost is $35,000 per lot, both inside the cited national bands. So the defensible claim is narrower than “no number works”: (1) at the 3.84–4.62 u/ac this site would realistically be approved for, no single input moving alone reaches $2.9M — only all three simultaneously; (2) every clearing case requires top-of-band values on inputs that are ASSUMED rather than researched, carries zero entitlement-risk discount, and assumes a rezoning actually happens; and (3) the entitlement is separately gated by the collector-access test in §2. A residual computed at bundled-optimistic inputs is a theoretical ceiling, not a bid any builder writes. But it would be dishonest to present the central case as the only case.
